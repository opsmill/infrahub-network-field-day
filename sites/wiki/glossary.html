TermMeaning
ApplicationA Helm chart plus values, deployed in the cluster.
ArtifactA file Infrahub renders from its data, such as a device configuration or the DNS zone.
BranchAn isolated copy of the Infrahub data where a change is made and reviewed before it merges.
GeneratorCode that reads a request and writes the technical objects it needs.
GrantA permission for a source network to reach an application. It becomes a firewall rule and a pod policy.
Proposed changeThe review of a branch before it merges into main.
TenantThe unit of isolation. A tenant has one VRF on the provider edge and may have several sites.
Virtual IP address (VIP)The address of a LoadBalancer Service, advertised to the fabric over BGP.
VRFA separate routing table. Two VRFs do not see each other's routes.
ZoneA group of firewall interfaces. Policies apply between a pair of zones.
OTTERNET lab. Served by nginx in the lab cluster.
