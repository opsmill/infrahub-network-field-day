The names, roles and contact details on this page are invented for the lab. No source describes OTTERNET as a company.
  1. Sign in to Infrahub as your own user, not as an administrator.
  2. Open the lab's demo runbook and read the section on the three gates: route, firewall, pod policy.
  3. Request OTTERNET wiki from the portal and watch the proposed change build.
  4. Find the new application's name in the address plan page of the network site.
  5. Resolve that name from the branch machine with nslookup.
  6. Withdraw the application and confirm the name stops resolving.
