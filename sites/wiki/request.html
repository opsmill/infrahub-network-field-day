The steps

  1. Open the Service Portal from the Firefox toolbar and sign in with Dex.
  2. Choose Exposed application, with access.
  3. Pick an application from the list, give it a name, and submit. The namespace is optional and defaults to the application name.
  4. The portal opens a branch in Infrahub and a proposed change. Generators add the address block, the firewall rule and the DNS name on that branch.
  5. Someone with merge rights reviews the proposed change and merges it.
  6. After the merge the application is deployed. Its name, <name>.int.otternet.lab, resolves from the branch machine about a minute or two later.

If it does not answer

  • The proposed change has not merged yet. Check Proposed changes in the toolbar.
  • The name does not resolve yet. The record reaches the resolver 15 to 75 seconds after the merge, then up to 30 seconds more to resolve.
  • The page times out but the name resolves. No access grant exists for your network, so the firewall drops the request.
